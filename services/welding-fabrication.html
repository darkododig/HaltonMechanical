Documentation & Compliance

When We Finish the Job,
You Get a Binder.

Most welding contractors do the work and walk away. We deliver a complete documentation package on every project, formatted for Ministry of Labour review. If an inspector walks in the next day, you're covered.

01

Written Weld Procedure Specification (WPS)

Every structural weld is performed to a written WPS — base material, filler, preheat, interpass temperature, and technique. No weld is run without a procedure behind it.

02

Material Mill Certifications

Material traceability from the steel service centre through to the finished weld. Mill certs for every structural member are retained and included in your package.

03

Post-Weld Inspection Report

Visual inspection and dimensional verification on every job. Where required, we coordinate third-party NDT (UT, MT, PT) and include reports in the deliverable package.

04

As-Built Drawings

Marked-up drawings reflecting the final installed condition. Essential for future modifications, permit applications, and ongoing facility records.

05

WSIB Clearance Certificate

Certificate of clearance issued with every project. Your facility's insurance obligations are protected throughout the work.
